What this check does NOT detect:
  - Whether answers are correct, factual or helpful. NLL rewards the likelihood of the reference answers, which are machine-translated model outputs: a gain can come from copying their style/translation artefacts, not from substance.
  - Regressions outside the held-out distribution (other tasks, other languages, safety, refusals, long context, multi-turn). Held-out rows come from the same dataset as train.
  - Paraphrased train/eval contamination: the leakage check in data prep is lexical only.
  - Behaviour under sampling (temperature/top-p) or other decoding settings: everything here is greedy or teacher-forced with repetition_penalty=1.0.
  - A deployment that uses a different runtime, chat template or system prompt (GGUF/Ollama/vLLM). Train and eval share Qwen's default system prompt, so a template error made consistently in both places cancels out and is invisible here.
  - Seed-to-seed variance: thresholds are fixed heuristics, not calibrated over several runs.
  - Small harmful changes on rare inputs; the probe set is ~12 prompts.
  - The 4-bit checks cover the bitsandbytes NF4 artifact only, not GGUF/AWQ/GPTQ exports.
